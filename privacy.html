Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 14, 2026

Welcome to Nihal Entertainment Television (the "Website"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or play our games.

1. Information Collection

We believe in privacy by default. When you visit our website:

  • We do NOT collect personal data: We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or physical location.

2. Third-Party Services

Our website uses services from trusted third parties. These services may collect anonymous data to help the website function:

  • YouTube (Google): We embed YouTube videos. When you watch a video, YouTube may store cookies or track viewing statistics according to the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Cookies

Our website itself does not use tracking cookies. However, third-party content (like YouTube videos) embedded on our site may use cookies to remember your video preferences.

4. Children's Privacy

Our content is safe for general audiences ("Family Friendly"). We do not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official YouTube Channel.
